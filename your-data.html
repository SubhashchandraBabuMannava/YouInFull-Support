Your context.
Your choices.

Source access, saved work and device recovery are different controls. Know which one you are changing.

Choose the sources you can read

Use Settings → Health data & sources in YouInFull to connect supported Apple Health information. Manage the permissions in Apple Health’s app-access settings.

Read permission allows YouInFull to read available source information. It is not permission to make a diagnosis, change a prescription or write over an original record. Apple keeps individual read-permission choices private, so an empty result does not prove that permission was denied.

Keep access to the original

Imported reports are retained as originals on this device. Select the saved record to inspect its original content and distinguish it from extracted text or a generated explanation.

Keep an independent copy of important original reports. YouInFull’s current protected document storage is excluded from device backup and is not an independent archive.

Keep or discard unfinished training

Closing a Training editor keeps your unfinished program, session entries and request wording on this device. In Training, choose the unfinished program to continue editing.

To remove that unfinished copy, open its editor and choose Discard unfinished edits. Read the confirmation before discarding. Your saved programs and saved questions remain.

When a request is still running, let it finish or stop it using its own controls before discarding its edits. If a Save needs checking, check its original result before trying again.

Return to an unfinished question

When you type a question about a food, recipe or exploration, its unfinished words stay on this device with that person’s workspace. Closing the screen keeps them. Open Questions & reviews to return to an unfinished food or exploration question.

To remove those local words, open the unfinished question and choose Discard unfinished words. Read the confirmation. Saved questions and their original requests remain.

Save question adds a question to your history. Keeping unfinished words does not ask a model for an answer. If an earlier Save needs checking, use its original checking control before making another change.

Understand what a change affects

Change read access

Changes what YouInFull can read from Apple Health. It does not erase the original from Apple Health or promise that every previously saved local object is deleted.

Edit or delete an item

Changes YouInFull’s saved state. Inspect the specific confirmation before deletion. Related interpretations may be invalidated or removed; an old result is not allowed to silently become current evidence again.

Resolve an editing conflict

Your draft stays separate from a newer saved version. Review both, choose explicitly and save. A deleted item requires an explicit new-item action rather than silent restoration.

Remove the app

Can lose the app’s local observations, investigations, memory, retained documents and other saved work. This is not a safe troubleshooting step in the current development build.

Sharing and removal

Review the exact selected content and intended recipients before exporting or sharing. Changing an app permission or deleting your local copy cannot retrieve an exported file from someone else.

In an enabled circle, check current members and the result of the original action. A connection failure can leave a result unknown. Use the action’s checking control before repeating it, and read removal confirmations for the copies they cover and any pending result. Removing a message from this device does not itself erase another member’s copy.

Block member hides that member across the circles in your current workspace on this device. Membership and saved originals remain. Blocked members in Circles provides an explicit Unblock choice even when the member is absent from current circles. A local block does not erase a recipient’s copy. Review blocking and mute, or read private-report and connection handling.

Keep your own original reports and an independent recovery archive before removing work you may need later. Review recipients and removal controls.

Restoring a Plus purchase is separate from restoring health records. It does not recover an archive key, another person’s workspace or circle access. Read membership and purchase restoration.

Before changing devices

Create an encrypted recovery archive in Settings → Recovery archives. Keep the archive outside this device and keep its recovery key separately. The key is needed to open the archive; YouInFull does not hold a copy or offer a way to reset it.

  1. Create the archive, deliberately reveal and retain its key, and confirm that you have kept it. If the app moves to the background during this step, enter the retained key again before continuing.
  2. Use the system share controls to save the encrypted archive to a location you control. An archive left only inside YouInFull on the source device is not an independent recovery copy.
  3. On the destination, choose the archive through Files and enter its key. Inspect the historical contents and any differences before confirming restore. Existing differences require an explicit choice to keep the current version; restoration does not silently overwrite them.
  4. Reconnect Apple Health and review source support on the destination. Device permissions, live source readings and reminder activation are not restored from the archive.

The archive preserves supported app-owned work, its revision history and retained original documents. Source-backed findings remain historical until their support is revalidated. An archive cannot include work saved after it was created; make a new copy after important changes.

Nearby transfer is still in development. Use an independently kept archive and key for a device change, and retain the original archive until restoration is complete. A selected task draft is separate from a recovery archive; receiving or opening a draft does not save its contents in the destination editor. Automatic synchronization is not provided.

The app’s working health store and retained documents are excluded from device backup. Automatic cloud backup and CloudKit synchronization are not provided. Apple Health’s own synchronization does not include YouInFull-owned observations, investigations, memory, plans or care preparation.

Do not delete or reinstall YouInFull while trying to recover saved work. Contact project support with a non-sensitive description. The maintainer cannot retrieve your local store, decrypt an archive or recover a missing key.
