Your context.
Your choices.

Source access, saved work and device recovery are different controls. Know which one you are changing.

Choose the sources you can read

Use Records to import a document or image you choose. Browse all saved sources and Search open previously retained originals. New clinical-service acquisition is outside this free release.

For an existing Apple Health source, read permission allows access to available records. It is not permission to make a diagnosis, change a prescription or overwrite an original. Apple keeps individual read-permission choices private, so an empty result does not prove that permission was denied.

Keep access to the original

Imported reports are retained as originals on this device. Select the saved record to inspect its original content and distinguish it from extracted text or a generated explanation.

Keep an independent copy of important original reports. YouInFull’s current protected document storage is excluded from device backup and is not an independent archive.

Keep or discard unfinished training

Closing a Training editor keeps your unfinished program, session entries and request wording on this device. In Training, choose the unfinished program to continue editing.

To remove that unfinished copy, open its editor and choose Discard unfinished edits. Read the confirmation before discarding. Your saved programs and saved questions remain.

When a request is still running, let it finish or stop it using its own controls before discarding its edits. If a Save needs checking, check its original result before trying again.

Return to an unfinished journal note

Closing a new Journal note keeps its words and unfinished input on this device, in the workspace for the person you are viewing. In Journal, choose Continue unfinished note to reopen it.

A tag you are still typing stays unfinished until you choose Add tag or clear that field. A partial scale definition stays with the note. Choose Continue unfinished scale to return to its entries; closing the scale editor keeps them. Invalid values remain editable.

Use scale applies the definition without filling in a rating. A rating stays unanswered until you enter or choose a value. Resolve unfinished tag and scale changes before saving the note. Save adds the note to your journal history. Keeping unfinished input does not ask a model for an answer.

If the app says your unfinished input could not be kept, keep the editor open. Choose Keep unfinished input again in the note or scale editor to check the same unfinished copy and try keeping your current entries. This action does not add a note to your history. A changed or removed unfinished copy is not silently replaced.

To remove only the unfinished note, open it and choose Discard unfinished note. Read the confirmation before discarding. Your saved journal entries remain.

Return to unfinished food and meals

Closing a meal, recipe or food-definition editor keeps its unfinished input on this device, in the workspace for the person you are viewing. In Nutrition, choose a meal under Unfinished meals. Open Your foods & recipes and choose a recipe under Unfinished recipes, or a food definition under Unfinished foods, to continue it.

Your words, ingredient choices and unfinished food input stay with that editor. Use its matching Continue control to return to a food search, label review, barcode match, photo choice or portion. Partial or conflicting amounts stay editable. Use applies a reviewed portion to the unfinished editor; Save meal, Save recipe or Save food saves the item. Saving a recipe or food definition does not record a consumed meal.

Choosing a photo or label image does not automatically read it or save a meal. Excluding an image changes whether it is included; its supplied original can remain with the unfinished input until you discard that copy. This does not delete the source photo or file.

If the app says your input could not be kept, keep the editor open. Choose Keep unfinished meal again, Keep unfinished recipe again or Keep unfinished food again to check the same unfinished copy and try keeping your current input. If a previous Save needs checking, use Check original Save before trying another Save or discard. Read its result and open the saved item when offered.

To remove the unfinished copy, choose Discard unfinished meal, Discard unfinished recipe or Discard unfinished food in its editor. Read the confirmation before discarding. When editing a saved item, discarding its unfinished changes leaves the saved item available.

Return to an unfinished question

When you type a question about a food, recipe or exploration, its unfinished words stay on this device with that person’s workspace. Closing the screen keeps them. Open Questions & reviews to return to an unfinished food or exploration question.

To remove those local words, open the unfinished question and choose Discard unfinished words. Read the confirmation. Saved questions and their original requests remain.

Save question adds a question to your history. Keeping unfinished words does not ask a model for an answer. If an earlier Save needs checking, use its original checking control before making another change.

Understand what a change affects

Change read access

Changes what YouInFull can read from Apple Health. It does not erase the original from Apple Health or promise that every previously saved local object is deleted.

Edit or delete an item

Changes YouInFull’s saved state. Inspect the specific confirmation before deletion. Related interpretations may be invalidated or removed; an old result is not allowed to silently become current evidence again.

Resolve an editing conflict

Your draft stays separate from a newer saved version. Review both, choose explicitly and save. A deleted item requires an explicit new-item action rather than silent restoration.

Remove the app

Can lose the app’s local observations, investigations, memory, retained documents and other saved work. Keep an independent encrypted archive and its recovery key before considering that step.

Export and older shared work

Review the exact selected content and intended recipient before exporting. A recipient or Files provider can retain a copy. Changing an app permission or deleting your local copy cannot retrieve an exported file.

New circles, invitations, shared-care connections and nearby transfer are unavailable in this free release. Previously saved originals and original service-operation records remain. A missing result does not establish whether an earlier action completed; preserve its identity and check that original before any retry.

Restoring local records is separate from an earlier Apple purchase or another person’s access. It does not recover a missing archive key. Read the export and earlier-sharing data flows.

Restoring a Plus purchase is separate from restoring health records. It does not recover an archive key, another person’s workspace or circle access. Read membership and purchase restoration.

Before changing devices

Create an encrypted recovery archive in Settings → Recovery archives. Keep the archive outside this device and keep its recovery key separately. The key is needed to open the archive; YouInFull does not hold a copy or offer a way to reset it.

  1. Create the archive, deliberately reveal and retain its key, and confirm that you have kept it. If the app moves to the background during this step, enter the retained key again before continuing.
  2. Use the system share controls to save the encrypted archive to a location you control. An archive left only inside YouInFull on the source device is not an independent recovery copy.
  3. Select the correct destination person, choose the archive through Files and enter its key. Inspect the contents and any differences before confirming restore. A wrong person is refused. If current work changes after the preview, review it again; restore does not silently overwrite newer edits or recreate known removals.
  4. Review existing source and reminder choices on the destination. Device permissions, live source readings and reminder activation are not restored from the archive.

The archive preserves supported app-owned work, its revision history and retained original documents. Source-backed findings remain historical until their support is revalidated. An archive cannot include work saved after it was created; make a new copy after important changes.

Use an independently kept archive and key for a device change, and retain the original archive until restoration is complete. Nearby transfer and automatic synchronization are not offered in this release. An archive cannot replace a missing required original with an empty copy.

The app’s working health store and retained documents are excluded from device backup. Automatic cloud backup and CloudKit synchronization are not provided. Apple Health’s own synchronization does not include YouInFull-owned observations, investigations, memory, plans or care preparation.

Do not delete or reinstall YouInFull while trying to recover saved work. Contact project support with a non-sensitive description. The maintainer cannot retrieve your local store, decrypt an archive or recover a missing key.
