Privacy, in plain view.

Different actions move different information. Here is what the current development build does.

What this notice covers

This notice covers the YouInFull development app and this support website, as described on October 7, 2026. YouInFull is not currently available on the App Store. Features and data flows will be reviewed again before a public app release.

The YouInFull maintainer can be contacted through project support on GitHub. That route is public and requires a GitHub account; use it only for non-sensitive questions. Use private security reporting for a security concern.

Health context stays in a protected local store

With your permission, YouInFull reads selected information from Apple Health, including supported clinical records. It also stores work you choose to save, including observations, journal entries, meals, workouts and plans, medication-use entries, care preparation, investigations, imported originals and their extracted text, findings and reviewed Health Memory.

In this build, the app does not upload this health store to a YouInFull server or synchronize it through CloudKit. The local store and retained documents use complete file protection on iOS and are excluded from device backup. Recovery is an explicit action: create an encrypted archive, keep it outside this device and keep its key separately. The destination must review and explicitly confirm restoration. Nearby transfer is still in development; use an independently kept archive and key for a device change. No YouInFull server receives the archive or its key. Keep an independent archive and its key separately; the maintainer cannot retrieve either for you.

Local search can include a limited text projection in Apple’s protected on-device Core Spotlight index. This system index is separate from the app’s database. Source changes and deletion are handled by the app’s indexing and evidence-update paths.

Optional intelligence and voice

Supported Apple Foundation Models tasks use the selected question and source context on your device. Model availability is checked for the particular task. Generated material is not automatically accepted as Health Memory, a fact or an adopted plan. You review it, and unsupported or unavailable interpretations remain distinguishable from source records.

A separate server-assisted route is in development. Before an available, qualified task can send information, the app identifies the selected source fields, YouInFull’s service and NVIDIA, and asks you to choose whether to send that request. The service processes the selected input in readable form to forward it to NVIDIA. This is different from encrypted circle delivery. Choosing one request does not authorize an upload of your complete health store or a future request.

The service retains operation identifiers, input commitments, usage and status to reconcile the original request, with an encrypted result for bounded retrieval. Its general operational logs do not contain the selected question, prompt, source text or generated output. This does not describe NVIDIA’s independent handling or promise that an already transmitted request can be recalled.

Current server qualification uses public reference material. It does not establish general availability of private health processing or paid server tasks. An unavailable task does not silently choose another provider. The app’s current recipient and scope review governs the available request.

Voice capture uses Apple’s SpeechAnalyzer and SpeechTranscriber after microphone permission. Transcribed words remain an editable draft. Apple model or speech assets may need to be downloaded; on-device processing does not mean the device never uses the network.

Membership and purchase restoration

Purchase options are not enabled in the current development app. The planned Free and Plus offer is described in Membership help. When purchasing is available, Apple handles the payment and presents the actual price, renewal period and eligible offer. YouInFull does not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account password.

The server membership implementation verifies Apple-signed purchase and app information. It keeps a separate financial membership identifier, hashes of the app-account and transaction identifiers, product, environment, verification time and entitlement expiry or grace state. A current device binding and verified subscription status are required for a new paid server operation. A copied health archive or client preference cannot grant Plus.

Restoring Plus follows the Apple Account used to purchase it, without restoring health records, recovery keys or circle access. Financial membership and health-person workspaces have separate purposes. Deleting a local health record does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple’s purchase history. Read purchase restoration and refund help.

Public-reference searches leave the device

When you explicitly search a public reference, the term or code you enter is sent to MedlinePlus Connect, DailyMed or PubMed/NCBI, depending on the search. These services also receive the connection information needed to handle a network request, such as your IP address.

The app does not attach your complete health store to these searches. However, text you type into a reference search is still sent, so do not enter your name, personal medical history or record identifiers. Returned reference information may be cached in protected local storage.

Food catalogue searches use the catalogue stored on your device. Additional food-detail downloads are still in development. When enabled, their download path requests public, signed reference files from GitHub Releases and its asset hosts; it accepts no food-search terms, meals, photos, account token or app device identity. GitHub receives the network information needed to serve the requested file, under its privacy practices. Downloaded reference files are cached locally.

Opening a source website is a separate action and uses that site’s own privacy practices. NLM describes its request metadata and search-term handling in its web policies. An ephemeral app network session does not control what a receiving service retains.

Private circles and selected reports

Circle connections are still in development. In an enabled connection, you review the selected content and current members before sharing. Shared words and selected files are encrypted for the addressed member devices. Those recipients can read and keep what they receive, including the complete selected files and their embedded metadata. The app does not send your complete health store as a circle post.

The connection service handles encrypted payloads and operational information needed to verify devices, check membership and deliver them. This includes pseudonymous profile, device and circle identifiers, public device keys, membership changes, request times and sizes, and the display alias you choose for a join request. Encryption does not hide that information from the service. Device verification and network requests also use the relevant Apple and hosting services. Do not put health details in a display alias.

Removing a local copy does not establish that another member’s copy was erased. A service removal can need its original result checked; an exported copy can remain outside the app. Read the sharing and removal choices.

Block member is a choice across the circles in your current workspace on this device. It hides that member’s posts and stops new direct invitation and approval actions involving them here, while retaining membership and saved originals. Mute this circle keeps its feed and changes this device’s notification preference. Remote notification delivery is still in development. These local choices do not remove another person from a circle or erase their copies.

Prepare a private report displays the selected member’s pseudonymous circle and device information. You individually choose any available message, activity or challenge excerpts and write your explanation. Review the complete report before choosing Copy this report. Copy keeps it on this device’s clipboard for five minutes; it does not automatically submit a report or include your health archive, attached files or other members’ messages.

The separate private GitHub reporting form requires a GitHub account. Its link carries no report text or identifiers. GitHub and the project participants authorized to handle the report receive what you choose to submit there, under GitHub’s privacy practices. Device clipboard expiry does not delete a report you submitted or a copy you pasted elsewhere. Keep the submission to what is needed to explain the concern; this is not a clinical support channel.

Reliability sharing is unavailable in this build

There is no production reliability-event transport configured in the current app. Builds without a sharing service do not send app reliability events or offer a sharing switch.

The intended event format is limited to app reliability outcomes and coarse technical context, not health values, documents, questions or record identifiers. No future collection service or server-retention promise is established by this notice. A changed implementation will need an updated disclosure before activation.

This website and support messages

This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. The site code sets no cookies, uses no analytics service, sends no analytics or help-search requests and stores no search history. Help search filters the topics already in this page without putting your search in a URL.

GitHub receives requests needed to serve the site and may process connection and usage information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This site’s local search behavior does not control GitHub’s hosting records.

Support issues you choose to post on GitHub are public and are handled by the YouInFull maintainer through that service. Closed discussions remain visible unless removed. Do not post health records, health details, identifying screenshots or credentials. Private security reports are limited to the security-reporting route and authorized repository participants; they are not a clinical support channel.

To ask the maintainer to remove information from a support post, identify the post without repeating its sensitive contents. GitHub separately controls its platform records and account privacy tools. This support route does not give the maintainer access to your local app data.

Your choices and retention

You choose Apple Health permissions and what you import or save. Local work remains on the device until you edit or delete it, or remove the app. Changes to source evidence can invalidate or remove dependent interpretations; user-authored reflections can remain with a visible indication that support changed or was removed.

Changing Apple Health read access is not the same as deleting local saved work or deleting originals from Apple Health. Deleting the app can lose YouInFull-owned local work. Recovery requires an archive you kept and its separate key. A support request cannot restore your local health store or replace a missing key.

Read Your data for these distinctions, or ask a non-sensitive privacy question through project support.
