Help with your
private journal.

Record or correct your day, find an original, and keep your work safe. Start with the task in front of you.

What are you working on?

Search stays in this page. No health records are searched or sent.

Write or correct a note

From Today → Add → Note or check-in, write what you noticed and choose its date. Save when you want it added to your history. No YouInFull account, subscription or Apple Health connection is required.

Open the saved entry to read or edit it. A blank rating stays unanswered. Dates, units and your own words keep their original meaning.

Find earlier journal entries

Open Health → Journal & patterns and choose All entries. Check By occurrence date and Without an occurrence date. Use Show earlier entries or Search for older work.

Open an entry and choose Edit entry to correct it. Earlier versions shows retained changes. A month-only date does not identify a particular day.

Record or edit a meal

From Today → Add → Meal, enter the meal and quantities manually. To change saved work, open Nutrition → Meal history, select the meal and choose Edit meal. Review the time, foods and portions before Save meal.

Inspect the nutrient basis and unit for each food. Unknown conversions do not supply a nutrient total. If a food reference is unavailable, keep a manual entry and the source details you know. A blank day does not mean no food.

Choose a routine and record progress

Use Today → Add → Routine or plan, or open Plans and your chosen practice. Write and review your routine. Keeping a draft does not activate it.

Open the saved routine’s Entries and history to record completion or a skip. A source measurement and an action you completed remain separate. Review a new revision before activating it; earlier history stays available.

Keep a record and inspect its original

Open Records, choose import and select a document or image through the system picker. Open Documents to find the retained original. Personal notes keeps your own context close.

An extracted passage or optional explanation is separate from the original. Keep important source files independently. Browse all saved sources and Search retain access to older saved material.

Return to unfinished input

Closing an editor can keep its unfinished words and fields in the same person’s workspace. Open the corresponding Journal, Nutrition, Training or practice collection to continue it. Read any retention message before leaving the editor.

If input could not be kept, use the editor’s retention retry while your words are still visible. If a Save needs checking, use Check original Save first. An unknown result is not permission to create another copy.

Discard removes the identified unfinished copy after confirmation. It does not delete an independently saved original. See Your data for the detailed distinctions.

Resolve changed or removed work

Your draft stays separate from a newer saved version. Inspect both, choose explicitly and then save. Keep the editor open while resolving a difference.

An old editor or archive cannot silently restore a known deleted item. Where offered, an explicit new-item action creates a separate identity. Check an unresolved original Save before retrying it or discarding its input.

Export and restore with the correct person

Open Settings → Recovery archives. Create an encrypted archive and use the system share controls to save it outside this device. Keep its recovery key separately and confirm that you have kept it.

Choose the destination person before opening the archive from Files. Enter its key, inspect the contents and differences, and explicitly confirm restore. A different person, changed preview or current edit must be resolved before restoration can proceed.

The working store is excluded from device backup. An archive left only on the original device cannot cover losing that device. YouInFull cannot recover a missing key and provides no automatic cloud backup. Read the recovery steps before deleting or reinstalling the app.

Use your journal offline

Manual notes, meals, routines and records already on this device remain useful without an account or network connection. Saved work and its source details do not depend on receiving an AI answer.

A public food-detail file that has not been downloaded may be unavailable offline. Enter the details manually or return to the reference later; the app does not invent missing nutrition. Files stored only with another provider may need to be made available on this device before import.

Read previously saved source records

Use Records → Browse all saved sources or Search to find retained records and their provenance. Existing Apple Health source readings keep their dates, units and original meaning. Missing readings are not recorded zeros.

This free release does not offer new clinical-service connections. You can add your own note or choose a source document from Files. Changing Apple Health access does not erase the original in Apple Health or replace a separately saved record.

Review routine reminders

Open Settings → Notifications to review this device’s choices and quiet hours. Refresh pending notifications checks the queue. Use Open notification settings if device permission is needed.

A reminder does not complete a routine or save an entry. Pausing reminders keeps saved schedules and history. Review reminder choices again after restoring an archive.

Use the free release

The selected release is free. Its local journal, meals, routines, records, export and encrypted recovery need no YouInFull account, subscription, payment card or trial.

There is no Plus purchase path in this release. Older saved work and original purchase or service records are retained. An unavailable optional capability does not require another purchase. Apple manages any earlier Apple-billed purchase separately from your local health records.

Open older saved work

Use Browse all saved work or Search to open previously saved plans, questions, experiments, care material and other originals. Their existing identity, history and source links are retained.

New shared-care, circle, clinical-service and companion-device acquisition are outside this release. A locally retained original is distinct from content that exists only on a service. If an original operation needs checking, preserve it and use its existing result controls; do not repeat a send or upload because its result is unknown.
