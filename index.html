What are you working on?

Search stays in this page. No health records are searched or sent.

Save a first observation

Open Timeline and choose Add an observation. Write what you noticed, check the date and choose Save. You do not need an account or Apple Health access to record your own words.

The editor closes after the save succeeds. Your observation belongs to the date you chose; open it again to read or edit it.

Understand missing Apple Health data

Open Settings → Health data & sources to connect the information you choose. Check the selected time period and the source’s last successful update.

Apple does not tell an app which individual read permissions you declined. An empty period can mean no readable observations, not necessarily denied access. Gaps are not recorded zeros.

Manage access in Apple Health → your profile → Apps → YouInFull. Changing access does not erase originals in Apple Health.

Find the original behind a value or report

Choose a measurement’s name or value to open its history and source details. Dates, units, methods and coverage explain what you are looking at; different measurement lanes use different scales.

For a document, open its record and inspect the retained original. Extracted or organized text is not a replacement for the original report.

Import and review a report

Choose Import a report on Timeline and select a document with the system file picker. YouInFull retains the imported original on this device.

Review any proposed organization against the original words and dates before saving it as Health Memory. A generated proposal is not automatically a health fact.

Keep your draft when a save conflicts

If an item changed while you were editing, keep the editor open. Inspect the saved version, then explicitly choose whether to keep your draft or use the saved version. Choose Save only after resolving the difference.

A removed item cannot be silently restored by an old editor. Where offered, saving your draft as a new item creates a separate identity.

Use your evidence when on-device answers are unavailable

Open Settings → Intelligence to check availability. A compatible device and available Apple model assets are needed for on-device explanations.

Your saved evidence, investigations, local search and exact comparisons remain useful without an answer. An interpretation may also be withheld when the selected records cannot reliably support it. Inspect the originals or use a smaller, more focused selection.

Know when a reference search goes online

Public-reference searches are different from exploring your own saved health evidence. When you request a reference, the entered term or code is sent to the selected National Library of Medicine service: MedlinePlus Connect, DailyMed or PubMed/NCBI.

Use a general term or code, not your personal history, name or identifiers. Read what these requests disclose.

Protect saved work before changing devices

Create an encrypted archive in Settings → Recovery archives. Keep it outside this device and keep its recovery key separately. On the destination, open the archive from Files, enter the key, review its contents and explicitly restore.

An archive left only on the original device cannot protect against losing that device. The app’s working store is excluded from device backup; automatic cloud backup is not provided. Reconnect Apple Health and review reminder choices after restoration.

Keep original reports independently too. Read device change and recovery before removing or reinstalling the app.
