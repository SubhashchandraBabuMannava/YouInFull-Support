Help with your
health context.

Record or correct your day, find an original, and keep your work safe. Start with the task in front of you.

What are you working on?

Search stays in this page. No health records are searched or sent.

Start or correct a workout

In Workouts, choose Start a workout. Manual timers do not create sensor readings. During an active session, use Pause recording, Resume recording or Finish workout as appropriate.

Open a recorded workout to correct an entered set with Edit this set, or use Add a set to this history where available. Set corrections stay in YouInFull; they do not rewrite recorded times or existing Apple Health copies. A planned session does not count as a recorded workout.

Create or adapt a training program

Open Training programs from Workouts. Choose Create program to write your own program, or Programs & exercises to browse examples. Review a proposal before choosing to keep it.

In a saved program, use Edit draft for a draft or Adapt program for an accepted program. Pause program keeps its planned dates and prescriptions. Resuming does not add catch-up work. Recorded sessions keep their saved prescription; changing a plan does not change what was performed.

Keep a route plan separate from a recorded route

Open Route plans from Workouts to inspect or create a plan. Use a recorded workout and its source to inspect the route actually recorded for that session. A planned map is not proof of where you travelled.

If a recorded route is missing, check the selected workout and connected source. No route data does not mean no activity. Keep the original activity file when you have one; review it before choosing Import activity file.

Correct a meal or portion

In Nutrition, use Meal history to find the saved meal, then choose Edit meal. Check its time, foods, selected portions and entered quantities before choosing Save meal.

Inspect the nutrient basis and unit for each food. An unknown conversion cannot supply a nutrient total; check the source or enter a compatible quantity. A blank day does not mean no food. To repeat an earlier meal, choose Repeat as a new meal and review the new time and portions instead of changing the original meal.

Find an entry or leave a journal field unanswered

Open Journal, select All entries and check both By occurrence date and Without an occurrence date. Use Show earlier entries for older work, or Search for your words. A month-only date does not identify a particular day.

Use Check in for a new entry. Open an entry and choose Edit entry to correct it; Earlier versions shows retained changes. Date, ratings and body locations are optional. An unanswered item stays missing, rather than becoming a zero or an absent symptom.

Understand a missing night or a source sleep assessment

Open Sleep & recovery and check the selected day and source. If the selected day has no episode, use Open latest recorded night where offered, or check sleep access in Apple Health. You can still choose Log how you feel without a recorded night.

Read recorded intervals and source assessments with their method and source. Different devices may describe the same night differently. Missing stages or a missing assessment do not establish that you had no sleep, and a source assessment does not replace your own observations.

Separate a prescription from confirmed medication use

In Medications, Prescriptions & recorded dose events shows source records. These records do not establish what you currently take. Choose Add medication-use entry to keep an account you have reviewed, or open an existing entry to edit confirmed use.

Use View & log to inspect the entry and record or correct a taken, skipped or not-recorded event. Review the original words, amount, time and schedule before saving. A missing event does not mean a missed dose. Do not infer a dose change or interaction assurance from an imported prescription or an app reminder.

Change reminders without changing your history

Open Settings → Notifications to inspect this device’s choices, preview settings and quiet hours. Choose Refresh pending notifications to check the queue. If device permission is missing, use Open notification settings.

Pause all reminders here keeps saved schedules and history. A reminder never records a dose, completes a care task, starts a workout or writes a check-in. Medication names appear only when you choose a named preview for that medication on this device. Review these choices again after changing devices.

Review a routine without treating completion as benefit

Open the saved routine and its Entries and history. Record what you chose to do separately from a source measurement or a result you observed. Missing or outdated source data does not count as a new completion.

Use Prepare a new revision when the routine needs to change, and review the revision before activating it. Earlier recorded history remains distinct. Completing an action alone does not show that it caused a health change.

Return to an interrupted request

Return to the original task and inspect its saved state before starting it again. A retained draft is work to review; opening it does not save a meal, workout, journal entry or care preparation. Review its sources and any differences, then save in the appropriate editor when you choose to keep it.

For an online action marked as needing a result check, use its checking control first. A dropped connection does not establish whether the original action completed. Keep independent work available while checking; do not repeatedly send the same action just because its result is unknown.

An encrypted recovery archive preserves saved work from its creation time. It is separate from a selected draft and from automatic synchronization.

Block a member or mute a circle

Circle connections are still in development. In the current circle controls, open the circle and its Members, then choose Block member. Review the confirmation: the choice applies across your circles in the current workspace on this device. That member’s posts become hidden and new direct invitations and approvals involving them are stopped here. Membership and saved originals remain.

Open Blocked members from Circles to choose Unblock member, including when the member is no longer in a current circle. A different person with the same display name is not automatically blocked. Use Mute this circle to change its notification preference while keeping its feed. Remote notification delivery is still in development.

A request already sent still needs its original result checked. Blocking is not member removal or erasure of another person’s copy. Read what the choices reach.

Review a selected private circle report

Use Prepare a private report from the selected member or displayed shared entry. Review the member and device information, choose individual excerpts when offered, and write only the explanation needed for the concern. Files, your health archive and other members’ messages are excluded from the prepared report.

Read Review the complete report before choosing Copy this report. The copy stays on this device’s clipboard for five minutes. Continue to private reporting opens the existing GitHub form without sending the report text in its link. A GitHub account is required; project participants handling the report receive what you choose to submit.

Nothing is automatically submitted. Do not paste the report into a public support issue. A submitted report or a copy pasted elsewhere is not removed when the device clipboard expires. Read private-report information handling.

Review recipients and what removal reaches

Before exporting or sharing, review the exact selected content and intended recipients. In an enabled circle, choose Check members before relying on a saved member list. If an original member action needs checking, use Check original result before repeating it.

Remove from this device removes a local copy. It does not establish that a recipient’s copy was erased. Read each removal confirmation for its scope and any result that still needs checking. A file already exported outside YouInFull can remain with its recipient. See sharing and removal.

Review an action opened from outside the app

When an available shortcut or other device surface opens YouInFull, check the selected person, task and draft before acting. Review proposed values in the editor and explicitly save when you choose to keep them.

If the task changed or was removed, open the app and choose current work instead of repeatedly invoking an older action. A preview or an opened draft does not prove that anything was recorded. Your other saved work remains available.

Save a first observation

Open Timeline and choose Add an observation. Write what you noticed, check the date and choose Save. You do not need an account or Apple Health access to record your own words.

The editor closes after the save succeeds. Your observation belongs to the date you chose; open it again to read or edit it.

Understand missing Apple Health data

Open Settings → Health data & sources to connect the information you choose. Check the selected time period and the source’s last successful update.

Apple does not tell an app which individual read permissions you declined. An empty period can mean no readable observations, not necessarily denied access. Gaps are not recorded zeros.

Manage access in Apple Health → your profile → Apps → YouInFull. Changing access does not erase originals in Apple Health.

Find the original behind a value or report

Choose a measurement’s name or value to open its history and source details. Dates, units, methods and coverage explain what you are looking at; different measurement lanes use different scales.

For a document, open its record and inspect the retained original. Extracted or organized text is not a replacement for the original report.

Import and review a report

Choose Import a report on Timeline and select a document with the system file picker. YouInFull retains the imported original on this device.

Review any proposed organization against the original words and dates before saving it as Health Memory. A generated proposal is not automatically a health fact.

Keep your draft when a save conflicts

If an item changed while you were editing, keep the editor open. Inspect the saved version, then explicitly choose whether to keep your draft or use the saved version. Choose Save only after resolving the difference.

A removed item cannot be silently restored by an old editor. Where offered, saving your draft as a new item creates a separate identity.

Use your evidence when on-device answers are unavailable

Open Settings → Intelligence to check availability. A compatible device and available Apple model assets are needed for on-device explanations.

Your saved evidence, investigations, local search and exact comparisons remain useful without an answer. An interpretation may also be withheld when the selected records cannot reliably support it. Inspect the originals or use a smaller, more focused selection.

On-device Apple tasks and server-assisted tasks have different recipients. A server request, when available, names YouInFull’s service and NVIDIA and shows the selected information before you choose to send it. The server route currently has public-reference development qualification; private health processing and paid availability remain unqualified. Read the intelligence data flows. An unavailable task does not require another purchase.

Know when a reference search goes online

Public-reference searches are different from exploring your own saved health evidence. When you request a reference, the entered term or code is sent to the selected National Library of Medicine service: MedlinePlus Connect, DailyMed or PubMed/NCBI.

Use a general term or code, not your personal history, name or identifiers. Read what these requests disclose.

Understand unavailable Plus options and the planned offer

Purchase options are still in development. YouInFull is not currently on the App Store. An unavailable offer does not require you to buy again or remove the app.

The planned U.S. launch offer is YouInFull Plus at $14.99 per month or $119.99 per year, with a 14-day introductory free trial for eligible new subscribers. Annual billing is $119.99 for the year. When purchasing becomes available, Apple will show your actual plan, price, trial eligibility and renewal terms before you confirm.

Your original records and saved work remain readable, editable, exportable and recoverable when membership ends. Existing medication reminders and an active workout do not stop because of billing. A new advanced task checks current access before it starts.

Manage renewal or cancel an Apple subscription

If you have a subscription billed by Apple, open your device’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions, select it and review the renewal or cancellation option. The app also provides Manage Apple subscription when commerce is enabled.

For a trial you do not want to renew, Apple advises cancellation at least 24 hours before it ends. Review Apple’s displayed expiration or renewal date; deleting the app is not a cancellation step. Read Apple’s cancellation instructions.

Restore a purchase or ask Apple about a charge

When purchases are enabled, use Restore Purchases in Membership if an existing purchase is missing. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase and check its receipt before purchasing again. See Apple’s guidance for missing in-app purchases.

For a charge billed by Apple, follow Apple’s refund-request instructions. Apple determines eligibility and the result. A pending charge cannot yet be selected for a refund request. Do not post a receipt, account credentials or payment details in a public support issue.

Protect saved work before changing devices

Create an encrypted archive in Settings → Recovery archives. Keep it outside this device and keep its recovery key separately. On the destination, open the archive from Files, enter the key, review its contents and explicitly restore.

An archive left only on the original device cannot protect against losing that device. The app’s working store is excluded from device backup; automatic cloud backup is not provided. Reconnect Apple Health and review reminder choices after restoration.

Keep original reports independently too. Read device change and recovery before removing or reinstalling the app.
